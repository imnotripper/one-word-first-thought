## Duel summary

#1 beats #2 and #9 on criterion 4 (its wit is in the act itself, not just the sound), beats #5, #6 and #8 on criterion 2 (it is the word nobody would guess as a first thought), and beats #3 on criterion 5 (it is chosen, not merely obscure — the image is instantly clear once you know it). No other candidate wins a criterion against #1. The word also survives the "first thought" framing: it is the kind of word that arrives uninvited, which is exactly what a first thought does.
